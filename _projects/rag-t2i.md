A RAG-fronted prompt-optimization stage for text-to-image generation: solves prompt mismatch of small LLMs against implicit knowledge with a two-stage retrieval pipeline (**Tavily + BGE-m3 + ChromaDB**), deployed on ollama + ComfyUI — **+114%** average score over LLM-only rewriting, without modifying the T2I model.

Published at the **WISE 2025 Workshop** (Tsinghua BNRist).
